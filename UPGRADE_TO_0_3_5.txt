ElectionLab -> 0.3.5
====================

This update is safe to apply directly from 0.1, 0.2, or 0.3.

1. CLOSE ElectionLab first.
2. Extract the complete 0.3.5 download.
3. Run Update_Existing_Install.bat from the extracted 0.3.5 folder.
4. Point it at your existing ElectionLab application folder.
5. Reopen ElectionLab and verify the footer says:
   Pre-1.0 build 0.3.5 · geographic map
6. Run an election and confirm the results screen shows the geographic U.S. map.

The updater preserves:
- portable_config.json
- .venv
- ElectionLabData
- Knowledge Vault profiles
- campaign saves/timelines
- research cache
- your selected external data root

0.3.5 does not intentionally migrate or rewrite your campaign/database schema.

Pre-1.0 still uses helper BAT files. The release target remains a normal
ElectionLab.exe + installer/updater with no console window, while large models
and mutable data remain placeable on F:/D:/another drive.
