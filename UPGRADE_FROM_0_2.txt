ElectionLab 0.2 -> 0.3.5
========================

1. CLOSE ElectionLab first.
2. Extract the complete 0.3.5 download.
3. Run Update_Existing_Install.bat from the extracted 0.3.5 folder.
4. Point it at your existing ElectionLab folder.
5. Reopen ElectionLab and verify the footer says "Pre-1.0 build 0.3.5 · geographic map".
6. Run Core_Self_Test.bat once.

The updater preserves your .venv, portable_config.json, ElectionLabData,
Knowledge Vault, saves, campaign timelines, research cache, and external data root.

0.3.5 includes all 0.3 GUI fixes plus the geographic state-boundary map.
